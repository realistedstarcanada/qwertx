Privacy Policy

How QWERTx collects, uses and protects your personal information when you create a watchlist, subscribe to QWERTx Pro, join an email list or contact us.

What we collect

If you subscribe, join the Pro early access list or contact us, we collect the details you give us, such as your name, email address and your answers to optional questions. We also collect basic, anonymous usage statistics to improve the site.

Your watchlist

When you create an account we store your email address, the ticker symbols you add, your alert settings and a record of the alerts we sent you. We do not ask for or store share quantities, prices paid, account numbers or brokerage logins. If you upload a CSV file, it is read in your browser and only the ticker symbols are sent to us. We never sell your watchlist or use it for advertising. You can delete your account and all of this information at any time from the account page.

Payments

QWERTx Pro payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe; we never see or store them. We keep your Stripe customer and subscription identifiers so we know your plan.

Service providers

Our site and database run on Cloudflare, emails are delivered by Resend and payments are handled by Stripe. They process information only to provide those services to us, and some of it may be stored outside Canada.

How we use it

We use your information only to send the emails you asked for, respond to your messages and tell you about QWERTx Pro if you joined the waitlist. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and preferences

When you sign in we set one essential cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 60 days. We store your light or dark theme choice in your browser. Third-party widgets (TradingView) and font providers may set their own cookies under their privacy policies.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from any email at any time, delete your account from the account page, and you can ask us to access, correct or delete your information by emailing support@qwertx.com.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@qwertx.com. We handle personal information in line with applicable privacy laws, including Canada’s PIPEDA where it applies.
