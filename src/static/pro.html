04 / FAQ

Questions about Pro

When does Pro launch?
Soon. Early access members hear first and get the founding-member offer.
Where does the data come from?
Official filings only: SEC 13F and Form 4 filings, House and Senate disclosures, and the President’s reports to the Office of Government Ethics. Every alert links to the source document.
Should I copy what super investors buy?
No. Filings arrive weeks after the trade and don’t show everything. Pro helps you notice and understand what smart money did; the decision stays yours.
Can I cancel?
Yes, anytime, from your account. Monthly plans stop at the end of the month you paid for; yearly plans at the end of the year.
