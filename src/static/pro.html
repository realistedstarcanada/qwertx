05 / FAQ

Questions about Pro

When will QWERTx Pro launch?
We’ll launch once our advisory partnerships and registrations are in place. Waitlist members will hear first.
Will Pro copy super investors’ trades?
No. 13F data is delayed and incomplete, so copying it blindly isn’t a sound strategy. Smart money research is one input; your portfolio will be built around your goals and risk profile.
How much will it cost?
Pricing isn’t final. We aim for transparent, all-in fees and will publish them clearly before anyone signs up.
Who will manage the portfolios?
Experienced financial advisors, operating through properly registered firms in each jurisdiction we serve.
