02 / Alerts you can trust

Strict rules, so every alert matters

Every alert shows the filing date and links to the official document. When a disclosure is old by law, we say so.

Fund managersSEC 13F

New, closed or meaningfully changed positions

You pick what “meaningful” means (1% to 50% of shares) and which managers to follow. 13F filings arrive up to 45 days after each quarter, and we show that lag on every alert.

InsidersSEC Form 4

Clusters of insider buying

Three or more different officers or directors buying on the open market within 30 days. Grants, option exercises and 10% owners are excluded.

PoliticiansSTOCK Act · OGE

When a disclosure lands

Members of Congress and the President, with both the trade date and the disclosure date, and the amount range exactly as reported.

Your controls

Instant or daily summary, thresholds, specific managers only, snooze, quiet hours and one-click unsubscribe. Earnings reminders are next on the list.

06 / FAQ

Questions about Pro

How fresh is the information?
As fresh as the law allows, and we always show the dates. Fund managers file 13F reports up to 45 days after each quarter ends. Members of Congress can disclose up to 45 days after a trade. Insider trades are reported on SEC Form 4 within two business days; our insider data currently refreshes when the SEC publishes its quarterly Form 4 data, and daily coverage is on the way. We check for new filings every hour and email you within about an hour of one appearing.
Do Canadian stocks work?
Partly. Fund managers’ 13F filings only cover US-listed securities, so we match Toronto listings to their New York listing when the company has one (RY.TO becomes RY, CNR.TO becomes CNI). Canadian-only stocks are shown as not covered rather than guessed.
Where does the data come from?
Official filings only: SEC 13F and Form 4 filings, House and Senate disclosures, and the President’s reports to the Office of Government Ethics. Every alert links to the source document.
Should I copy what super investors buy?
No. Filings arrive weeks after the trade and don’t show everything (no short positions, cash or most non-US holdings). Pro helps you notice and understand what smart money did; the decision stays yours.
What does the founding price include?
The first 500 members pay US$99 a year instead of US$144, and keep that price for as long as their subscription stays active. If you cancel and come back later, the regular price applies.
Can I cancel?
Yes, anytime, from your account page. Monthly plans stop at the end of the month you paid for; yearly plans at the end of the year.
